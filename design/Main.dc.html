The amber itself never changes — #FFB020 is the same byte in both themes, and buttons use it as-is either way. Light needs two darker relatives beside it, because amber on paper is too pale to read as text (accentInk) or to hold its own as a bar (accentGraphic). In dark both collapse back to the accent.
