Colours for the charts
The app design has no charts in it, so this set is new. Seven slots, assigned to a category in fixed order and never recycled — a category keeps its colour when a filter changes, which is what stops a chart lying to you between two views. Amber, mint and coral are deliberately absent: they already mean button, succeeded and failed.

Rules that come with the palette
Colour follows the category, not the rank. Slot order is fixed per category id. Filtering to a shorter list must not repaint the survivors.
An eighth category folds into Uncategorised rather than getting a generated eighth hue.
Every wedge is named in text. Two slots sit just under 3:1 against their card, so the label carries identity and the colour only confirms it.
Unmapped spend keeps the 115° hatch from the app design. It is drawn, never hidden — an unnamed payee is the thing the dashboard most needs to admit.
Both sets pass the lightness-band, chroma, colour-blind separation, normal-vision separation and contrast checks against their own surface — checked, not eyeballed.
