Delete your account and data

Use Delete account in the app to sign out and erase the app’s local data on your device. We do not keep a central account or financial-data database and cannot perform this deletion remotely.

Developer: Sandeep Chorge
Android app: Statement Sense / Transactions Parser

Delete in the app If you cannot use the app Data and retention

Deletion is permanent. The in-app action removes local data for every account used on this device, not just the account currently signed in. It does not delete your Google account.

Delete from within the app

  1. Open Statement Sense and select the You tab in the bottom navigation bar. This opens Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of Settings and tap Delete account, below Log out.
  3. Read the “Delete account and local data?” confirmation, then tap Delete account. Tap Cancel if you do not want to proceed.
  4. Wait for deletion to finish. The app stops active imports, clears local records, profile details, preferences and caches, and clears its Google sign-in state.
  5. After successful deletion, you are signed out and returned to the Google login screen. Signing in again starts with an empty local account unless you restore a backup.

If deletion fails: some data may already have been removed. Use Delete account again to retry, or contact support below.

If the option is unavailable: finish or cancel any export, backup or restore in progress. If the option is missing, update the app or use the Android storage controls described below.

Log out only ends your session and preserves local records. Use Delete account for the complete local reset.

If you cannot use the app

If you cannot open the app, use your phone’s Settings → Apps → Statement Sense → Storage & cache → Clear storage (sometimes called Clear data), or uninstall the app. Android removes the app’s private local data; button names vary by device. Clear cache alone does not remove your account or database.

Deletion must be performed on your device. Sending an email does not delete the app’s local account or data, and support cannot access or erase that storage remotely.

If you already uninstalled the app, Android has removed its private local data; you do not need to reinstall it solely to delete that local copy. Manage Android backups and exported files separately, as an older backup may restore data when the app is installed again.

What gets deleted and what may remain

Scope of the in-app Delete account action
DataWhat happens
Transactions and statement historyAll parsed transactions, sessions and upload logs are permanently removed from the device’s app database, including recently deleted records.
Payees and categoriesPayee names, aliases, mappings, identifiers and categories are permanently removed for every local account.
Profile and preferencesLocally stored name, mobile number, gender, Google sign-in details, dashboard choices and appearance settings are cleared.
App caches and local diagnosticsApp-owned cached files are removed. Local analytics data and crash-report identity are reset, and deletion of unsent crash reports is requested through Firebase.
Original PDFs and exported filesOriginal statements, exported CSVs and JSON backups remain where you saved them. Delete these separately using the relevant Files or cloud-storage app.
Android backups and other devicesSystem backups and copies on other devices are not erased by this action. Manage them through your Android/Google backup settings and each device.
Previously uploaded analytics and crash reportsThese are not deleted by the local reset. They remain subject to the Google/Firebase retention periods described below.
Your Google accountYour Google account and its data remain active. To remove the app’s Google authorization separately, visit Google account connections and remove Statement Sense or Transactions Parser.

Deletion timing and retention

  • Local app data: removed when the in-app deletion completes, with no recovery or retention period in the app. This includes items in Recently deleted.
  • Firebase Analytics: our privacy policy states a retention period of 2 months for user-level event data, with aggregate reporting retained longer. This is a retention policy, not a promise that the in-app action deletes previously uploaded records.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Google retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems. See Firebase’s privacy and retention information.
  • Exports and external backups: retained according to your own storage and backup settings until removed separately. We cannot set or enforce their retention period.

Statement parsing happens on your device. The app uses Google sign-in, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, and Android may back up app data. Deleting local data does not automatically delete copies held by those services.
